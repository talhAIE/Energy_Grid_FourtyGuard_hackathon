The Idea, End to End FortyGuard Hackathon '26
Energy Grid Heat-Demand Forecaster
In one line: Software that watches the weather and warns the power grid before it gets overwhelmed by everyone turning on their AC at once.
▼ ▼ ▼ ▼
Objective
Give power utilities enough advance warning
about heat-driven electricity demand that they
can act before things go wrong — not after.
Turn FortyGuard's hyperlocal (city-block-level)
temperature data into: "Zone 4 will spike in the
next few hours — here's how much, and how long
you have to prepare."
Problem It Solves
Heat drives AC use — demand isn't spread
evenly across a city.
Grids have limits — sudden demand forces
emergency power or blackouts.
Today's forecasting is too broad — city-wide,
not district-by-district.
Reaction beats prevention — by the time
demand rises, the calm window to act is gone.
How It Works
1 Heat data
FortyGuard current + 12hr forecast per
zone
2 Cooling Degree Days
proven utility formula, not a guess
3 Calibrate
tune with public demand datasets
4 Risk score
0–100 per-zone demand-spike score
5 Agent loop
observe→evaluate→plan→validate→act→record,
human approves
Why We Stand Out
Named vertical — Energy Trading is one of
FortyGuard's 3 named 2026 growth focuses.
Real science — built on a documented,
industry-proven formula.
Hard to fake — most teams won't attempt a
real model.
Boardroom stakes — blackout prevention gets
judge attention.
Team fit — we have the AI depth to build the
real version.
Why it's not just a chart: A dashboard displays numbers. Our system continuously watches, reasons, and recommends action to a human — that's what makes it an AI agent, matching the hackathon's primary track.
